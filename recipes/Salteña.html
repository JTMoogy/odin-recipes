Salteña

The "Salteña" is an iconic Bolivian empanada —filled with meat, egg, potato, peas, olives, and spicy chili— with a history spanning more than two centuries within Bolivian gastronomy. This delicious empanada serves as a culinary symbol that brings millions of people in Bolivia together; it is both a dish for special occasions and a source of livelihood through local businesses.

The uniqueness of the salteña lies in its filling made with native Bolivian ingredients, such as chili peppers, potatoes, and a stew prepared with beef foot broth.

Ingredients

Ingredients for the dough:

  • 4 cups of flour
  • ½ cup of lard colored with annatto (urucú or achiote)
  • ½ cup of sugar
  • ¾ cup of water
  • 1 egg

Other ingredients

  • ½ kg ground beef, browned
  • 1 cup finely chopped onion
  • 3 tablespoons oil
  • 6 tablespoons ground red chili (ají colorado)
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1 tablespoon oregano
  • 2 tablespoons red food coloring powder
  • ½ liter beef broth
  • 2 cups cooked, cubed potatoes
  • 1 heaping tablespoon unflavored gelatin
  • ¼ cup sugar
  • 2 tablespoons chopped parsley
  • Salt to taste
  • Hard-boiled quail eggs

Steps

  1. In a pan, melt the lard over low heat.
  2. Add the urucú (annatto) and stir constantly until the lard takes on the color of the annatto.
  3. Remove the annatto and let the lard cool until it solidifies.

Preparing the salteña dough

  1. Place the flour on a work surface and mix it with the achiote-infused lard until the mixture has a sandy texture.
  2. Create a well (a "volcano" shape) in the mixture and add the sugar, salt, and egg to the center, then gradually add the water.
  3. Mix everything thoroughly and knead until the dough is firm and well combined.
  4. Divide the dough into small balls and cover them with a plastic bag.
  5. Refrigerate the dough balls for approximately 20 minutes.

Preparing the "jigote" (filling) for "salteñas"

  1. In a pan with hot oil, sauté the onion until translucent.
  2. Add the ground meat and brown it; then add the ground red chili ("ají colorado"), cumin, red food coloring, beef broth, sugar, and salt.
  3. Cook over medium heat for 25 minutes, until the "jigote" thickens slightly.
  4. Remove from the heat, sprinkle the unflavored gelatin over the mixture, and stir well.
  5. Add the cubed potatoes, chopped parsley, and oregano. Adjust the salt if necessary and mix thoroughly. Then, let it cool.
  6. Cover the mixture and refrigerate until the "jigote" sets.

Assembling the salteñas

  1. Remove the dough balls from the refrigerator and roll them out with a rolling pin until you have medium-thick, oval-shaped pieces of dough.
  2. Place a spoonful of the prepared filling ("jigote") and one hard-boiled quail egg in the center of each piece of dough.
  3. Brush the edges of the dough with beaten egg, close the salteña, and press down to remove any air before crimping the edges.
  4. Arrange the salteñas on a baking sheet, brush them with beaten egg, and bake at 350°C for 10 to 15 minutes.
