Api con Pastel

Pastel, dessert combines the softness of a spongy cake with the characteristic sprinkled sugar and Api, a hot drink based on purple corn and flavored with cinnamon and cloves.

Ingredients

  • 4 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons hot butter
  • 1 tablespoon of sugar
  • ½ tablespoon of salt
  • Required amount of hot water
  • ¼ kg Mennonite cheese

Steps

  1. In a container, place the flour forming a volcano and add the sugar, the salt, the hot water in the center and the hot butter previously melted until it begins to smoke on the water.
  2. Mix from the center outwards with the help of a spoon until you obtain a homogeneous dough. Knead for 2 minutes. If the dough is too soft, you can add a little more flour to make it firmer.
  3. Form balls of the appropriate size for your cupcakes.
  4. Cover the balls and let them rest for 30 minutes; This will help bubbles form in the dough.
  5. Roll out each ball of dough to form a large, as flat oval as possible. Add grated cheese to one side of the dough.
  6. Brush the edges of the dough with a little cold water and close the cupcakes, pinching the edges or pressing them with a fork to seal them.
  7. Heat oil in a frying pan over high heat and cook the cupcakes. You will notice that they puff up as they cook, at which point you should turn them over.
  8. Remove the cupcakes when they are golden brown on both sides and sprinkle ground sugar on top before enjoying.
